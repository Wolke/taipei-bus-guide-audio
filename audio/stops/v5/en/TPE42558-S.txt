Shilin Police Precinct (士林分局) is our next stop. Nearby, Shilin Cixian Temple (士林慈諴宮) sits within the well-known Shilin Night Market. Also called Shilin Mazu Temple, it is dedicated to Mazu, the Heavenly Holy Mother. Its history began in seventeen ninety-six. The temple has five courtyards, with halls and side wings arranged in a traditional southern Fujian style. Its doors feature finely painted guardians and palace attendants.
