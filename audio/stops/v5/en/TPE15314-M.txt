Executive Yuan (行政院) is our next stop. Just one hundred and forty-three meters from the stop is Yixian Park (逸仙公園), a small park near Taipei Main Station dedicated to Sun Yat-sen.

There’s a story behind this quiet memorial setting. In nineteen thirteen, Sun Yat-sen stayed at the Japanese restaurant that stood here during his visit to Taiwan. It was also where he met Taiwan’s governor-general, Sakuma Samata, to discuss political affairs.

The park covers about three thousand and twenty-five square meters. Its main building is a rectangular, Japanese-style structure with a roof of dark tiles. Built in nineteen hundred, the restaurant took its name from the plum trees planted in its garden.

In its restaurant days, this was an exclusive gathering place. Governors-general, business leaders, and prominent figures in society were among its guests. So this little park connects a memorial to Sun Yat-sen with a glimpse of Taipei’s social life in the early twentieth century.
