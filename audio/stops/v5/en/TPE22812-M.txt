Next stop, Xinyi and Yongkang Street Intersection (信義永康街口).

Just seventy-three meters from this stop is the Yongkang Street Area (永康街區), a neighborhood where homes, culture, and food come together.

Its story reaches back to the Japanese colonial period, when an irrigation network and nearby government residences helped draw people here. Later, schools, colleges, and cultural institutions helped shape the neighborhood we know today.

Look at the older buildings, and you’ll find that some now house tea shops, bookstores, and cafés. That mix of old structures and everyday modern life is part of the area’s character.

Around Yongkang Park (永康公園), shopfronts, old window frames, plants, and the changing light at lane corners are popular subjects for photographs.

There are also small design shops and tea houses along Lishui Street (麗水街) and parts of Jinhua Street (金華街). From afternoon into evening, the neighborhood is especially suited to a leisurely wander and a taste of its food.
