Next stop, MRT Jiantan Station, Zhongshan (捷運劍潭站). Just one hundred meters away is Taipei Performing Arts Center (臺北表演藝術中心), where theater gets creative. Its three theaters can work separately or connect into one large performance space. And here’s a lovely detail: the Public Loop winds through the building, revealing hidden stage corners and routes used by performers and staff. There’s also an open viewing platform overlooking the Shilin area.
