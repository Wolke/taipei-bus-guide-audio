Taipei Main Station, Zhongxiao (臺北車站忠孝), is our next stop. Just over a hundred metres from the stop is one of Taiwan’s major railway hubs, with its distinctive square building and soaring main hall.

There’s much more here than platforms and timetables. Beneath the station, Taiwan Railway, Taiwan High Speed Rail, and the Taipei MRT’s Bannan and Tamsui-Xinyi lines come together. Walking connections also link the station with intercity buses and the Taoyuan Airport MRT.

And beneath your feet is a whole network of underground shopping streets. You’ll find bookstores, places to eat, clothing shops, and souvenirs. It’s a transport hub woven into everyday city life.

The station’s story began on July fifth, eighteen ninety-one. Since then, it has moved and been rebuilt several times, reflecting Taipei’s growth and the changing railway network. Today, its high ceilings and open central spaces give this busy meeting point a bright, spacious feel.
