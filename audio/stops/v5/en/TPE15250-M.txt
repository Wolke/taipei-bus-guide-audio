Taipei Main Station, Zhongxiao (臺北車站忠孝), is our next stop.

About one hundred and ninety meters away, Taipei Main Station brings rail travel, shopping, and everyday city life together. Its distinctive square building has a high ceiling in the main hall and an open central space, giving the interior a bright, airy feel.

Below ground, Taiwan Railways, Taiwan High Speed Rail, and the Taipei MRT’s Bannan and Tamsui-Xinyi lines meet. The nearby bus station and Taoyuan Airport MRT add even more connections.

There is plenty here beyond trains. Underground passages link shops selling books, clothing, and souvenirs, along with places for a meal or a quick snack.

The station’s story began on July fifth, eighteen ninety-one. Since then, it has moved and been rebuilt several times, reflecting Taipei’s growth and the changing railway network. Today, that long history sits alongside the practical rhythm of ticket counters, luggage storage, shopping, and travelers on the move.
