Zhongxiao Dunhua Intersection (忠孝敦化路口) is our upcoming stop. This is Taipei’s East District, where fashion, food, and nightlife share the spotlight. Let’s take a closer look at two nearby places, one among the streets and lanes, and one beneath them.

The East District Shopping Area (東區商圈) is about two hundred and twenty meters from the stop. Its nightlife has a five-part theme: eating, exploring, entertainment, fashion, and a quieter, arts-minded side. So an evening here does not have to mean just shopping or dancing.

Look to the lanes for the food scene. Creative restaurants sit within a district whose choices range from traditional Taiwanese snacks to international cooking. Shops and cafés stay lively as evening arrives, while boutique hotels are also part of the neighborhood. On weekends, bars and nightclubs draw young people and office workers for music, dancing, and relaxation.

Fashion brings another layer, with international labels, high-end boutiques, designer names, limited-edition goods, and emerging independent brands. For a gentler evening, some cafés and bars offer an artistic atmosphere rather than a busy party scene.

About two hundred and thirty-two meters from our stop is East Metro Mall (東區地下街). This bright, spacious underground shopping passage runs seven hundred and twenty-five meters between MRT Zhongxiao Fuxing and Zhongxiao Dunhua stations. It has seventeen entrances and seven plazas, linking shopping areas above with shops below.

Inside, the selection stretches from everyday goods and clothing to accessories, cosmetics, electronics, and food. But there is a local history detail worth noticing, too. At entrances associated with Dinghao and Longmen plazas, waterfall and fountain decorations draw on the area’s cultural and geographical character, including its connection with the Liugong Canal. Those water features bring a reference to local history into a thoroughly urban shopping setting.

Together, these two places show the East District’s range: lively lanes above, and a connected shopping world below.
