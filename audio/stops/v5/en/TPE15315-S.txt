Taipei Main Station (臺北車站), Zhongxiao, is our next stop. Nearby, Taipei Main Station is one of Taiwan’s major railway hubs. Its distinctive square building has a lofty main hall, while Taiwan Railways, Taiwan High Speed Rail, and Taipei MRT services meet below. Beneath the station, underground shopping areas offer books, food, clothes, and souvenirs. It’s a place where travel, shopping, and everyday city life all come together.
