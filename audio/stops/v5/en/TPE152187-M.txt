MRT Daan Park Station (捷運大安森林公園站) is our next stop. Just over one hundred thirty meters away is Daan Park (大安森林公園), a green space right in the city center.

Known as the city’s lungs, this forest park covers nearly twenty-six hectares. There’s plenty of variety within that greenery, from bamboo groves to palms that give the southeastern corner a tropical feel.

Over in the northwest, a natural pond has small planted islands and koi swimming through the water. It’s a lovely scene to pause and enjoy.

At the center, an outdoor music stage has gently sloping audience space for nearly a thousand people. Fragrant plants, including gardenias and osmanthus, grow around it.

Tree-lined paths connect the park, with flower beds and pavilions along the way. There’s also a children’s play area. In the mornings and toward sunset, nearby residents come here to walk, exercise, dance, and enjoy this patch of urban forest.
