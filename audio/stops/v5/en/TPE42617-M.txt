Fulin Elementary School (福林國小) is our upcoming stop. Nearby, Fulin Park (福林公園) offers a little greenery with an artistic twist, just one hundred and seventy-one meters from the stop.

This triangular park sits where Zhongshan North Road meets Fulin Road in Shilin District. Completed in nineteen ninety-one, it covers about two point six hectares.

Its special feature is a collection of six abstract sculptures inspired by nature. They represent the sun, clouds, mountains, a waterfall, water, and a well. Their shapes turn pictographic images into three-dimensional art. Here’s a detail to look for: the water sculpture is cast in bronze, while the other five are made from natural stone.

The sculptures are spread throughout the park, and their shapes create different impressions when viewed close up or from farther away. Around them, green lawns and leafy trees bring a touch of the countryside into the city. It’s a place where nature and sculpture share the scene.
