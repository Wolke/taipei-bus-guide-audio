Nanjing and Longjiang Road Intersection (南京龍江路口) is our next stop. Nearby, two everyday objects have quite a story to tell.

About ninety-six meters from the stop are the Leaning Mailboxes (歪腰郵筒). In twenty fifteen, Typhoon Soudelor brought powerful winds and a falling sign that left these red and green mailboxes bent in the same direction. They look rather like a pair of people leaning sideways together! Photos spread online, turning them into an unusual local landmark. And they are not just for pictures. After inspection and repairs by Chunghwa Post, both kept their original job of accepting mail.

About two hundred and thirty-two meters away, Liaoning Park (遼寧公園) offers a different scene. Tall fan palms give it a tropical touch, while leafy banyan trees provide shade. Flower beds and gentle slopes add variety to the landscape. There are benches, a flower-covered walkway, and a children’s play area with playground equipment and rocking toys.
