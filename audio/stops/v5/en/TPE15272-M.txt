Next stop is Nanjing Dunhua Intersection (南京敦化路口), by Taipei Arena (臺北小巨蛋).

About one hundred and forty-four meters from this stop, Taipei Arena brings sports, performances, and ice skating together under one roof. This was once the site of Taipei Municipal Baseball Stadium. The old stadium was demolished in late two thousand, making way for a multipurpose arena with fifteen thousand seats.

Inside, the main venue can change its layout for different events. Even its central floor can become an ice surface for competitions. That flexibility makes it a popular setting for major sporting events and performances by groups from Taiwan and overseas.

There is also a dedicated skating rink, described as Taiwan’s largest meeting international standards. It welcomes recreational skaters and serves as a training venue for competitive athletes, including national team members. Its ice can accommodate four hundred skaters, with seating for another eight hundred spectators. Quite a different sporting world from the baseball field that stood here before!
