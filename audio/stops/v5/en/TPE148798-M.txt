Xinyi–Yongkang Street Intersection, MRT Dongmen Station (信義永康街口，捷運東門站), is our next stop.

Just fifty-four meters away is the Yongkang Street Area (永康街區), a birthplace of Taipei’s lane and alley culture. This neighborhood brings homes, culture, and food together in one setting.

Its story goes back to the Japanese colonial period, when an irrigation system and nearby government residences helped draw people here. Today, many older buildings remain, with some finding new life as tea houses, bookshops, and cafés.

There’s plenty to notice beyond the food. Old shop signs, vintage building fronts, and little pockets of greenery give these lanes their character. Around Yongkang Park (永康公園), shopfronts, old window frames, plants, and the light at alley corners are popular subjects for photographs.

The neighborhood changes its mood from daytime into evening. Afternoon through early evening is especially suited to a leisurely walk and a taste of the local food.
