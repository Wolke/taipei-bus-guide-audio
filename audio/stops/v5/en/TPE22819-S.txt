MRT NTU Hospital Station (捷運台大醫院站) is our next stop. Nearby is Two Two Eight Peace Memorial Park (二二八和平公園), where old trees, Japanese-style ponds, and arched bridges offer a gentler view of Taipei. You may see local residents playing chess or practicing tai chi. The park also has a solemn side. Its memorial monument and museum commemorate the February twenty-eighth incident, with the monument expressing hopes for peace and reconciliation.
