MRT Daan Park Station (捷運大安森林公園站) is our next stop. Just one hundred and seventy-two meters away is Daan Park (大安森林公園), a green space right in the city center, often called the city’s lungs.

There’s quite a variety of scenery here. Near the main entrance, flower beds and leafy plants lead into an area of Buddhist sculpture and, farther south, groves of several kinds of bamboo.

In the northwest corner, a natural pond has small planted islands and koi swimming through the water. It’s a lovely spot for watching the fish.

At the heart of the park, an outdoor music stage has gently sloping seating for nearly a thousand people. Fragrant plants, including gardenias and osmanthus, grow around it.

The southeast has a different feel, with palms creating a tropical landscape. Across the park, tree-lined paths, resting pavilions, and a children’s play area offer space for strolling, relaxing, and playing amid the greenery.
