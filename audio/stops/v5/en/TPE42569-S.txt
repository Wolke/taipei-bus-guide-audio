Next stop, Wesley Girls’ High School, Indigenous Peoples Cultural Park (衛理女中，原住民文化主題公園). Nearby is the Shung Ye Museum of Formosan Aborigines (順益台灣原住民博物館), Taiwan’s first private museum devoted to Indigenous peoples. Look for its gray, trapezoid-shaped building, colorful glass decoration, and central stone pillar carved with Indigenous motifs. Inside, four collection themes explore traditional life, from clothing and hunting tools to crafts, architecture, and beliefs honoring ancestral spirits.
