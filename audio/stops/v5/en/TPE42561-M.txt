Fulin Elementary School (福林國小) is our next stop. Just over a hundred meters away is Fulin Park (福林公園), a green space with a little outdoor art to discover.

Completed in nineteen ninety-one, this triangular park covers about two point six hectares in Shilin District. Its distinctive feature is a collection of six abstract sculptures inspired by nature: the sun, clouds, mountains, a waterfall, water, and a well.

Here’s an interesting detail: the water sculpture is cast in bronze, while the other five are made from natural stone. Their designs turn pictographic forms into three-dimensional artworks. Seen from a distance or close up, their shapes and colors offer different impressions.

Around these works, lawns and leafy trees give the park a countryside feeling within the busy city. It’s a place where greenery and sculpture share the scene, with something different to notice as your viewpoint changes.
