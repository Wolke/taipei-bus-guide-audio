MRT Zhongxiao Dunhua Station (捷運忠孝敦化站) is our next stop. This part of Taipei brings together fashion, food, and nightlife, with another shopping world running beneath the streets.

About two hundred and ninety-five meters from the stop is the East District Shopping Area (東區商圈). Its evening character is built around five themes: eating, exploring, enjoying nightlife, fashion, and reading. That means there is more here than a row of shops. The neighborhood offers several different ways to spend an evening, from music and dancing to a quieter café with an artistic atmosphere.

Look beyond the main shopping streets to the lanes. Restaurants here range from traditional Taiwanese snacks to international cooking, with creative food choices tucked among the businesses. Fashion has a similarly wide range. International labels and luxury boutiques share the district with designer collections, limited-edition goods, and emerging independent brands.

After dark, shops and cafés remain lively. On weekends, bars and nightclubs are especially busy with young people and office workers. But the district is not only about late nights. Shopping and dining are part of its daytime appeal, too.

Around three hundred and ten meters from our stop is East Metro Mall (東區地下街). This bright, spacious underground shopping street stretches seven hundred and twenty-five meters, linking this MRT station with MRT Zhongxiao Fuxing Station (捷運忠孝復興站). It has seventeen entrances and seven plazas, connecting shopping areas above with the passage below.

Inside, the selection runs from everyday household goods and clothing to accessories, beauty products, electronics, and food. There are curated small goods and design brands as well.

For a detail with a local story, look for the waterfall and fountain decorations at entrances beside the connected plazas. These artworks draw on the area’s setting and cultural history, including Liugong Canal (瑠公圳). They bring a reference to the neighborhood’s waterway into this busy shopping landscape.
