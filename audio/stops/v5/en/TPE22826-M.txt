Xinyi and Jianguo Intersection (信義建國路口) is our next stop. About two hundred and thirty meters away is Jianguo Holiday Flower Market (建國假日花市), where a parking area beneath the overpass takes on a very different character on weekends and holidays.

Across more than eight thousand square meters, over two hundred stalls turn this space into a city garden. You’ll find roses, orchids, leafy plants, and little succulents, along with fresh flowers, seeds, tea, and gardening supplies. There are flowerpots to browse, too, and the vendors can share their plant knowledge and growing experience.

It’s not only about shopping. The market draws tens of thousands of visitors each week, including people who come simply to enjoy the flowers. Occasionally, street artists sketch portraits here, adding another kind of creativity among the plants. There are also hands-on activities held from time to time, giving parents and children a chance to learn and have fun together.
