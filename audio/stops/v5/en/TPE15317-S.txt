MRT Ximen Station (捷運西門站) is our next stop. Just seventy meters away, by exit six, is Rainbow Six (6號彩虹). This rainbow landmark was created in September twenty nineteen. Earlier that year, Taiwan became the first country in Asia to legalize same-sex marriage. The rainbow celebrates Taipei’s commitment to human rights, gender equality, and a welcoming city for all genders. It has also become a popular spot for photos.
