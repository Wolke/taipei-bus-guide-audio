Yangming Theater (陽明戲院) is our next stop. Nearby, Shilin Night Market (士林觀光夜市) is Taipei’s largest night market and a lively part of the city’s nightlife. People come for shopping and especially the food! You’ll find giant fried chicken cutlets, Shilin sausages, juicy pan-fried buns, and warming herbal pork rib soup. The market spreads through streets around the theater, with another main section on Jihe Road. There’s plenty here to tempt your appetite.
