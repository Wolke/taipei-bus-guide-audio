Nanjing and Jianguo Intersection (南京建國路口). Just two hundred and twenty-five meters away is the Miniatures Museum of Taiwan (袖珍博物館). Opened in March, nineteen ninety-seven, it was Asia’s first museum dedicated to contemporary miniature art. Inside, more than two hundred themed displays include dollhouses and detailed room scenes. Here’s a delightful detail: wine bottles just one centimeter tall contain real brandy, and paintings the size of postage stamps are made with real oil paint.
