MRT Nanjing Fuxing Station (捷運南京復興站) is our next stop. Nearby, Liaoning Park (遼寧公園) has tall fan palms around its edges, giving it a touch of tropical character. Flower beds and gentle grassy slopes add variety to the landscape, while established banyan trees offer shade. There’s also a children’s play area with playground equipment and rocking rides. Benches and a flower corridor round out this green space, about two hundred and forty-six meters from the stop.
