Nanjing and Longjiang Road Intersection (南京龍江路口) is our next stop. Here’s a nearby sight with a rather unusual story: the Leaning Mailboxes (歪腰郵筒), just one hundred and one meters from the stop.

These red and green mailboxes stand on Longjiang Road in Zhongshan District. In twenty fifteen, Typhoon Soudelor swept across Taiwan. Strong winds and a falling sign left both boxes bent in the same direction, almost like two people leaning sideways together.

Their unexpected pose caught the attention of passersby, who stopped to take photos with them. Pictures spread online, and the pair became a popular, out-of-the-ordinary photo landmark.

But there’s more to these mailboxes than their crooked looks. They also carry a piece of local history. And they’re not simply there for photographs: after Chunghwa Post assessed and repaired them, both kept their original purpose. These storm-bent boxes can still accept mail, giving this little landmark a useful everyday role.
