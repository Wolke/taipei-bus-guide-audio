Nanjing Jianguo Intersection (南京建國路口) is our next stop. Nearby is the Miniatures Museum of Taiwan (袖珍博物館), about one hundred and seventy-seven meters from the stop.

Founded in nineteen ninety-seven, it was Asia’s first museum devoted to collecting contemporary miniature art. Inside, more than two hundred themed displays turn tiny spaces into whole worlds.

There are dollhouses showing buildings inside and out, and room boxes that capture individual rooms in remarkable detail. The scenes range from ancient Roman ruins and romantic Venice to Buckingham Palace and the fairy-tale world of Snow White.

And here’s the lovely part: the materials are real, just wonderfully small. A wine bottle only one centimeter tall holds actual brandy. An oil painting the size of a postage stamp is painted in real oils.

Many pieces follow a scale of one to twelve. Even something as small as a key on a desk has its place in these carefully crafted little worlds.
