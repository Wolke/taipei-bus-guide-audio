Next stop, MRT Jiantan Station (捷運劍潭站), by Taipei Performing Arts Center (臺北表演藝術中心). Just sixty-nine meters away, this creative hub brings art into everyday life. Its three theaters can work separately or connect to create a larger performance space. Here’s a special feature: the Public Loop winds through the building, revealing hidden corners of the stage and routes used by performers and crew. There’s also an open viewing platform overlooking the surrounding Shilin area.
