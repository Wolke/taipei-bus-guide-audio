MRT Songjiang Nanjing Station (捷運松江南京站) is our next stop. Nearby, about two hundred and thirty-seven meters away, is Siping Sunshine Shopping District (四平陽光商圈).

If you’re familiar with Taiwan’s night markets, this area offers a different rhythm. It has some of that market atmosphere, but it opens during the daytime. Along its short pedestrian street, you’ll find outdoor café seating, with the scent of coffee drifting through the air.

Look above the street, too. Fabric flags in four colors flutter overhead, adding a little romance to the scene. From time to time, street performers bring an unexpected bit of entertainment, though performances are not a constant feature.

The district has also been welcoming more international visitors. Shopkeepers can use simple English and Japanese to communicate with overseas guests. It’s a daytime shopping area where cafés, colorful flags, and occasional performances all share the same pedestrian space.
