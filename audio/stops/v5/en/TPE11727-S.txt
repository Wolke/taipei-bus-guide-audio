Two Two Eight Peace Park (二二八和平公園) is our next stop. Nearby, the National Taiwan Museum (國立臺灣博物館) stands inside the park. It’s Taiwan’s oldest museum, and the building itself is a national historic monument. Greek architectural features meet a Roman-style dome, with grand columns and delicate carvings. Inside, sunlight shines through stained glass overhead. Collections of native plants, animals, minerals, and cultural objects offer a glimpse of Taiwan’s rich natural and human heritage.
