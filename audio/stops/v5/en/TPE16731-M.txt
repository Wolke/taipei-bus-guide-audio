Our next stop is MRT Shilin Station, Zhongzheng (捷運士林站中正). Nearby is Shilin Shennong Temple (士林神農宮), about two hundred and forty-eight meters from the stop.

This temple has a fascinating story behind its roof. It was originally dedicated to Fude Zhengshen, the earth god. During rebuilding in the Qianlong era, local residents gave it elegant swallowtail roof decorations. But those were reserved for temples honoring deities of imperial rank. To avoid having the temple demolished for breaking that rule, residents made Shennong, an imperial-ranking deity, its main god.

Today, Shennong occupies the center of the main hall’s upper floor, while the earth god is honored directly below.

Despite several rebuildings, the temple still preserves stone dragon columns, carved stone windows, and incense burners from the Qing dynasty. There is also a special pavilion where paper bearing written characters was burned, a reminder of the respect for learning in Shilin’s earlier days.
