Fulin Elementary School (福林國小) is our next stop. Just over a hundred metres from the stop is Fulin Park (福林公園), a green space with a nature-inspired art collection.

Completed in nineteen ninety-one, this triangular park covers about two point six hectares. Its sculptures turn familiar scenes from nature into abstract, three-dimensional forms, drawing on the shapes of pictographic characters.

There are six groups to look for: the sun, clouds, mountains, a waterfall, water, and a well. Here’s a detail worth noticing: the water sculpture is cast in bronze, while the other five groups use natural stone.

These artworks are spread throughout the park, with changing shapes, colours, and spaces creating different impressions from nearby and farther away. Around them, lawns and leafy trees give the setting a countryside feel within the busy city. It’s a place where greenery and sculpture share the scene, offering different ways to enjoy the same surroundings.
