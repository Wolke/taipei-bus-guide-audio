Zhonghua Road North (中華路北站) is our next stop. Here, shopping, photography, and postal history are all close by.

About two hundred meters away is Ximending (西門町). Its name dates from the Japanese colonial period, when the area outside Taipei’s west gate was a favorite leisure spot. Cinemas helped shape its early commercial life. Today, it brings together fashion, food, youth culture, and street performances. On holidays, its streets and covered walkways fill with crowds.

Nearby, Camera Street around Bo’ai Road and Hankou Street (博愛路、漢口街相機街) has Taiwan’s greatest concentration of photographic equipment shops. The businesses grew with customers from government offices and newspapers, helped by easy access to Taipei Main Station.

Then there’s Taipei Beimen Post Office (臺北北門郵局), less than three hundred meters from this stop. Rebuilt in reinforced concrete in nineteen twenty-nine after a fire, this four-story building has an arched entrance and a two-story-high main hall, with delicate classical carvings on its columns and ceiling.
