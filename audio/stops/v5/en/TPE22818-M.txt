Taipei Main Station, Zhongxiao (臺北車站（忠孝）), is our next stop. Welcome to one of Taiwan’s major railway crossroads!

About a hundred and ninety meters from this stop is Taiwan Railways Taipei Station (臺鐵臺北車站). Its distinctive square building has a lofty main hall and an open central space, giving this busy transport hub a bright, spacious feel.

Below ground, Taiwan Railways, Taiwan High Speed Rail, and the Taipei MRT’s Bannan and Tamsui-Xinyi lines come together. The Taoyuan Airport MRT and Taipei Bus Station are also within walking distance.

But there’s more here than trains. The underground shopping areas offer books, clothing, souvenirs, and plenty of places to eat. It’s a whole layer of city life beneath the streets.

The station’s story began on July fifth, eighteen ninety-one. Since then, it has moved and been rebuilt several times, reflecting both Taipei’s growth and the changing story of Taiwan’s railways.
