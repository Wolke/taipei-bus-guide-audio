National Taiwan Science Education Center (國立臺灣科學教育館) is our upcoming stop. This part of Shilin brings science and family fun close together, with two very different ways to spend a curious afternoon.

The science center is only about eighty-three meters from the stop. Its guiding idea is simple: we understand more when we try things ourselves. Rather than leaving science on a page, the center brings it into models, demonstrations, interactive equipment, and simulated environments.

The permanent exhibitions occupy the third through sixth floors. Their subjects range from life science to matter and energy, Earth systems, mathematics, and technology. That means there is room to follow different interests, whether you are curious about living things or the principles behind everyday physical events.

One especially memorable example is the aerial bicycle. A bicycle on a high wire becomes a way to explore balance. Earthquake experiences take a different approach, using moving platforms to simulate different shaking intensities and build understanding of disaster preparedness. Younger children have a discovery area designed around their learning needs.

The learning also extends beyond the exhibition floor. Workshops offer activities such as model making, materials experiments, and science crafts. Changing special exhibitions introduce further themes across nature, technology, and engineering.

About three hundred and twenty-six meters from our stop is Taipei Children’s Amusement Park (臺北市立兒童新樂園). Here, thirteen major rides sit alongside free play areas and experiences planned for different ages.

The Ferris wheel is a visual highlight, offering views across the park and Shilin. Around dusk, the lights add another dimension to the scene. There are also themed performances and a seasonal water play area.

For families, practical details matter too. Nursing rooms, accessible routes, and resting places support a more comfortable visit. EasyCard and iPASS can be used for admission, rides, and purchases at designated shops. Together, these nearby attractions pair hands-on discovery with shared moments of play.
