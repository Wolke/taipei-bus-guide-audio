Next stop, Xihu Library, Huguang Church (西湖圖書館（湖光教會）).

Just ninety-six meters from this stop is the Neihu Seven Three Seven Shopping District (內湖737商圈). This short stretch has two different sides to its day. In the morning, Lishan Market (麗山市場) serves local residents shopping for groceries. In the evening, the area becomes a destination for food, with Taiwanese snacks and restaurants packed into a relatively compact district.

It draws visitors as well as office workers looking for something to eat after work. So while the street may not be long, there is plenty of choice for anyone curious about Taiwanese food.

For those exploring Taipei by MRT, the district is also about a ten-minute walk from exit one at Gangqian Station (港墘站). Nearby, Huguang Church adds a European-style architectural touch to the surroundings. Together, the morning market, evening food scene, and neighboring church give this corner of Neihu its own distinctive character.
