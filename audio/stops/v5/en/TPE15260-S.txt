Linsen North Road One (林森北路一) is our next stop. Nearby, Beyond Gallery (非畫廊) connects Taiwan’s contemporary art with the wider world. Founded in January two thousand eight, it introduces works by important international contemporary artists while promoting talented Taiwanese artists and exhibitions overseas. It also builds relationships with museums, curators, artists, and collectors around the globe. The idea behind its name is simple: to be more than just a gallery.
