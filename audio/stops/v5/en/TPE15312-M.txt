Nanjing–Linsen Road Intersection (南京林森路口) is our next stop on route three hundred and seven.

Nearby are Linsen and Kangle Parks (林森、康樂公園), about one hundred and thirty meters from the stop. These green spaces sit on opposite sides of Linsen North Road, near its junction with Nanjing East Road in Zhongshan District.

Here’s a little history behind the flowers. During the Japanese colonial period, this area had old, single-story homes. A group of military veterans lived here for many years. Later, the city worked with residents through public participation to improve the surroundings. The result became Taipei’s first urban garden.

Today, the parks bring greenery and colorful flowers into this busy part of the city. To picture the layout, Kangle Park is on the side toward Section Two of Zhongshan North Road. Linsen Park is toward Section Two of Xinsheng North Road. Together, they offer a green contrast to the bustle around this intersection.
