Next stop, MRT Jiantan Station (捷運劍潭站), by Taipei Performing Arts Center (臺北表演藝術中心). This creative hub brings the arts into everyday life. Its three theaters can work separately or connect to create a much larger performance space. Here’s a special feature: the Public Loop takes visitors through hidden corners of the building and routes used by performers and crew. It also includes an open viewing platform overlooking the surrounding Shilin area.
