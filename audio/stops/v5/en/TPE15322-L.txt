Wanda Road (萬大路) is our upcoming stop. This part of Wanhua brings together clothing design, neighborhood greenery, and a night market whose name remembers a very different past.

About three hundred meters from the stop is Xiyuan Twenty-nine Fashion Design Base (西園29服飾創作基地), formerly the Taipei Fashion Institute. This three-story red-brick building connects emerging designers with the surrounding clothing trade. Its guiding idea is a tree: roots drawing nourishment from Wanhua’s history, with creative branches reaching into the neighborhood.

Inside, the white ground-floor space presents fashion alongside a coffee bar, design publications, and videos. The second-floor studios are not normally open to visitors. Upstairs, here is a lovely detail: the ceiling tracks for movable partitions follow the pattern of Bangka’s streets.

That connection continues in the Bangka Clothing Shopping District (艋舺服飾商圈), around four hundred meters away. Wanhua’s early development and convenient railway access helped a major garment market grow here. At its height, it had nearly two thousand shops, linking design, patternmaking, production, and sales. The tourism description records around four hundred remaining, with clothing for mature women making up roughly four-fifths. Delivery trucks and wholesale buyers are part of its everyday rhythm.

For a greener contrast, Heping Herb Garden (和平青草園) occupies a roughly triangular site once used by Renji Sanatorium and homes. Opened in November two thousand six, it preserved healthy existing trees. Near Xiyuan Road are aromatic and medicinal herbs. Lawns and shade trees provide space for neighbors to relax and chat.

Finally, Nanjichang Night Market (南機場夜市) carries an aviation story in its name. A Japanese military airfield once stood in this area. Military dependents’ housing and public housing followed, and the growing population helped the market develop. Today, the attractions include chicken rice, sesame-oil chicken, freshly made pan-fried buns, oyster fritters, and tofu pudding. From an airfield to a neighborhood food destination, that is quite a transformation.
