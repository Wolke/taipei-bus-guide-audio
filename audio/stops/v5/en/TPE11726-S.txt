Chongqing South Road, Section One (重慶南路一段), is our next stop. Nearby is Chongqing South Road Book Street (重慶南路書店街), a longtime gathering place for book lovers. In its heyday, more than a hundred bookstores and publishers lined this road. Writers, scholars, and students could happily spend an afternoon browsing here. There are fewer bookstores today, but this is still Taipei’s most concentrated stretch of them. A little street with a big literary history!
