National Palace Museum (故宮博物院) is our next stop. Nearby, this Chinese palace-style museum was completed in nineteen sixty-five. Its collection holds nearly seven hundred thousand objects, spanning almost five thousand years of Chinese history. That’s quite a journey through art! Exhibition spaces fill the first three floors, while the fourth has a teahouse for a quieter moment. Among its best-known treasures are the Jadeite Cabbage and the Meat-shaped Stone.
