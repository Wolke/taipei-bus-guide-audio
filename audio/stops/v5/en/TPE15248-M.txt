Zhonghua Road North (中華路北站) is our next stop. There’s quite a mix nearby, from photography shops to youth culture and historic architecture.

About two hundred meters away is Bo’ai Road and Hankou Street Camera Street (博愛路、漢口街相機街). This area has Taiwan’s highest concentration of photography equipment shops. The trade grew with customers from nearby government offices and newspapers, helped by convenient access to Taipei Main Station.

Then there’s Ximending (西門町), whose name dates back to the Japanese colonial period. Cinema was an early mainstay here, followed by department stores and other entertainment. Today, it brings together youth fashion, street performances, and food ranging from Taiwanese dishes to international flavors.

Nearby, Taipei Beimen Post Office (臺北北門郵局) offers a different mood. Rebuilt in reinforced concrete in nineteen twenty-nine after a fire, this four-story building has an arched entrance and a hall rising through two floors. Inside, classical carvings add elegant detail to the columns and ceiling.
