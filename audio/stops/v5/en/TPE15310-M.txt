MRT Songjiang Nanjing Station (捷運松江南京站) is our next stop. Just one hundred and ninety meters away is Siping Sunshine Shopping District (四平陽光商圈), a short pedestrian street with a daytime personality.

It has something in common with a night market, but here, the activity happens during the day. Open-air cafés line the walking area, with the aroma of coffee adding to the atmosphere. Look above the street and you’ll see fabric flags in four colors fluttering in the breeze. They give this little stretch a playful, romantic feel.

Street performers sometimes make an appearance, too, bringing an unexpected moment of entertainment. Their performances are occasional, so they’re a surprise rather than a regular feature.

The district has been welcoming more international visitors, and shopkeepers can communicate in simple English and Japanese. It’s a daytime shopping area where cafés, colorful flags, and occasional performances share the same pedestrian space.
