Taipei City Hospital, Heping Branch (聯合醫院和平院區), is our next stop. About two hundred meters away stands Xiaonanmen, also called Chongxi Gate (小南門，重熙門). Here’s a little piece of old Taipei: the city once had five gates, rather than the usual four. The walls are gone, but this gate remains. Its tower was later rebuilt in a northern Chinese palace style, while the base still keeps its original appearance.
