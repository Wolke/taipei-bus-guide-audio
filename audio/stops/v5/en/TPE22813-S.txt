Next stop, MRT Dongmen Station (捷運東門站). Nearby are the Taipei Prison Wall Remains (臺北監獄圍牆遺蹟), a city-designated historic site. The prison dates back to around the nineteen tens, during Japanese rule. Today, only sections of its northern and southern walls remain. Here’s a detail worth noticing: much of the stone came from Taipei’s old city walls. These surviving walls offer a glimpse into the history of Taiwan’s modern prison system.
