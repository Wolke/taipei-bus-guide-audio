Our next stop is Fulin Elementary School (福林國小). Nearby is Fulin Park (福林公園), just one hundred and seventy-one meters from the stop.

This triangular park sits at the junction of Zhongshan North Road and Fulin Road. It opened in nineteen ninety-one and covers about two point six hectares. In this busy part of the city, its green lawns and leafy trees offer a touch of the countryside.

The park’s special feature is a series of six abstract sculptures inspired by nature: the sun, clouds, mountains, a waterfall, water, and a well. Their shapes turn pictographic images into three-dimensional art. Here’s a detail to notice: the water sculpture is cast in bronze, while the other five are made from natural stone.

These artworks are spread throughout the park. Their forms and colors create different impressions depending on whether you see them from a distance or take a closer look.
