Next stop, MRT Shilin Station, Zhongzheng (捷運士林站，中正).

About two hundred and fifty meters from the stop is Shilin Shennong Temple (士林神農宮). Its architecture tells a fascinating story about local devotion and imperial rules.

The temple originally honored Fude Zhengshen, the earth god. During rebuilding in the Qianlong era, residents gave it a swallowtail roof, a style reserved for deities of imperial rank. To keep the temple from being demolished for breaking those rules, they made Shennong, a deity of that higher rank, its main god.

Today, Shennong occupies the center of the main hall’s upper floor, while the earth god is honored directly below.

There are plenty of details to appreciate, too: stone dragon columns, carved stone windows, and colorful glazed pottery decorations. The temple’s Shennong figures have either red or black faces. Red represents his healthy appearance after introducing grain farming; black recalls his sacrifice in tasting poisonous plants while searching for medicinal herbs.
