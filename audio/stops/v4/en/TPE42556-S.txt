Next stop is MRT Jiantan Station, Taipei Performing Arts Center. Just nearby is the Taipei Performing Arts Center (臺北表演藝術中心), where three theaters can work separately or join together as one larger performance space. Here’s a lovely detail: its Public Loop takes visitors through hidden backstage corners and routes used by performers and crew. There’s also an open viewing platform with views across the surrounding Shilin area, offering another way to enjoy this creative space.
