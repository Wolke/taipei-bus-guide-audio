Next stop, Yangming Theater (陽明戲院). Nearby is Shilin Night Market (士林觀光夜市), the largest night market in Taipei. The streets around the theater are part of this sprawling destination for food and shopping. There’s plenty to tempt your appetite, from giant crispy chicken cutlets and fragrant Shilin sausages to juicy pan-fried buns and warming herbal pork rib soup. Alongside the snacks, all sorts of unusual goods draw crowds into this much-loved part of Taipei’s nightlife.
