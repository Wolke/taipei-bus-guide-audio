Shilin Precinct (士林分局) is our next stop. Nearby is Shilin Cixian Temple (士林慈諴宮), also known as Shilin’s Mazu Temple, right within Shilin Night Market. Dedicated to Mazu, the temple has five courtyards and a traditional southern Fujian layout. Look for the finely painted figures on its doors, created by the craftsman Chen Yu-feng. Beside the main hall, a stone wall depicts Shilin’s old streets, offering a glimpse of the neighborhood’s past.
